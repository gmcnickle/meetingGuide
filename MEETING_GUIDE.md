# 🧭 Meeting Etiquette Guide
This guide outlines best practices for organizing and running effective meetings. It’s designed to reduce wasted time, increase clarity, and foster productive collaboration. These conventions are not hard rules—but they reflect principles that help teams work better together.

## Guiding Principle
Make meetings meaningful. Encourage productivity—not waste it.

While these conventions are not rigid rules, those marked **Strongly Advised** should only be broken with serious consideration. All team members are expected to support and uphold these practices to foster team alignment and work satisfaction.

## When Not to Meet

Before scheduling, run through this quick check. If **any answer is No**, don't schedule — find another path.

| Question | If **No**, then… |
|----------|------------------|
| Do I have a clear purpose or outcome for this meeting? | Don't meet. Write it down and share instead. |
| Do I need real-time input or decisions from multiple people? | Consider async collaboration — email, chat, or doc comments. |
| Can this be handled with a quick update or FYI? | Use a message or post, not people's calendars. |
| Will more than 50% of attendees speak or contribute? | Rethink the invite list — or cancel. |
| Is this blocked by something that isn't done yet? | Wait. Then schedule only if it's still needed. |

> **Rule of thumb:** If the meeting exists mainly to share information, share it another way.

## Guidance

### Scheduling & Duration

- ✅ Do keep meetings as short as possible.
- 🤔 Consider scheduling meetings in **25- or 50-minute** blocks to allow for transition and reflection.
- ✅ Do include **breaks** in any meeting longer than 50 minutes—and **honor** them.
- ⚠️ Avoid meetings longer than **2 hours**.
- ❌ Do Not schedule meetings exceeding **3 hours**.

---

### Agenda & Timekeeping

- ✅ Do include a **clear agenda** in the calendar invite, including expected break times.
- ✅ Do ensure the **invite reflects the full duration** of the planned or implied meeting.
- ✅ Do enforce **hard stops** at scheduled break points. Resume only after the break.
- ❌ Do Not let meetings **run past their scheduled end**, even briefly. End on time, and if needed, **schedule a follow-up**.
- ✅ All attendees are empowered to **hold the team accountable** to the documented agenda and break schedule.

---

### Attendance & Participation

- ✅ Do invite **only those expected to actively contribute**.
- ⚠️ Avoid inviting participants for **informational or training purposes** only.
- ✅ Do share meeting outcomes (notes, recordings, etc.) with others **afterward** via your team's collaboration platform.
  - Tip: Use your platform's **meeting notes** or **chat** features during the meeting. After it ends, share a summary or forward access to recordings and shared content.

---

### Tools & Facilitation

- ✅ Do use your **organization's standard collaboration platform** for all meetings. Avoid introducing alternative platforms that require extra setup or learning.
- ✅ Do designate a **facilitator** to guide the meeting and keep it on topic.
  - If the meeting veers off-course, **redirect discussion or schedule a new meeting** for unrelated topics.
